Robert Marc

Madison Avenue independent. Architectural frames from a NYC optician turned designer.

The House

Robert Marc — the story.

Robert Marc built his reputation as one of New York's great independent opticians, with a run of Madison Avenue boutiques that became a destination for clients who wanted eyewear treated as seriously as tailoring. The house collection grew out of that optician's-eye view: frames designed by someone who has fitted thousands of faces.

The design language is architectural New York — clean lines, considered proportions, sophisticated two-tone laminations and muted colorways that read as texture rather than statement. Frames are produced in premium acetates and titanium with Japanese-level finish work.

It's a collection for the client who wants something clearly designed, clearly independent, and completely wearable — the frame equivalent of a perfectly cut blazer.

Signature Piece

What we'd put on your face first.

Any of the two-tone laminated rectangles or pantos — shapes that flatter in the mirror and photograph even better.

Why we carry it

The shop's take.

Because the optician-turned-designer pedigree shows. These frames fit beautifully out of the tray, and the NYC design sensibility travels well to the island.
