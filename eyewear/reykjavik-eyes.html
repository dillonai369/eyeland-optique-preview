Reykjavik Eyes

Icelandic minimalism. Screwless titanium frames at nine grams.

The House

Reykjavik Eyes — the story.

Reykjavik Eyes came out of Iceland with a Nordic design brief taken to its logical conclusion: pure titanium frames, no screws, no welds, no soldering — assembled entirely through engineering rather than hardware.

The frames weigh around nine grams, flex without fatigue, and are hypoallergenic by nature of the material. The palette is Icelandic — volcanic charcoals, glacier blues, moss greens, quiet metallics — and the shapes are clean, geometric, and deliberately unfussy.

For clients who love the Lindberg idea but want a different accent, this is the other Nordic answer: same engineering-first philosophy, its own distinctly Icelandic character.

Signature Piece

What we'd put on your face first.

Any of the screwless titanium rounds in a volcanic matte finish — glasses you forget you're wearing by the second day.

Why we carry it

The shop's take.

Because featherweight screwless titanium shouldn't only mean one brand. Reykjavik Eyes brings the Nordic minimal look at a friendlier price point than the big Danish houses.
