About the shop.

An independent optical boutique and full-scope eye care practice inside the Fresh Market Shoppes — and, by public vote, the island’s best eyewear shop three years running.

Who we are

Independent, on purpose.

Eyeland Optique pairs a comprehensive eye exam with a frame collection you will not find anywhere else on Hilton Head Island. Lindberg from Denmark, Chopard from Switzerland, l.a. Eyeworks, Orgreen, Lunor, Vuarnet — handmade, independent houses chosen one piece at a time.

The practice combines more than 40 years of optometric expertise and master opticianry. Every exam uses current diagnostic technology, and every frame is fitted by an ABO-certified optician. Clients can book a private personal-shopping appointment to take their time with the collection — a habit that began out of necessity and became the way we prefer to work.

Outside the shop, the team supports Pockets Full of Sunshine, the Hilton Head Choral Society, The Children’s Center, and the Palmetto Bluff Women’s Association.
