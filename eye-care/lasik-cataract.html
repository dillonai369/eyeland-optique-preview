LASIK & Cataract Co-Management

We handle the workup, refer you to the right surgeon, and manage the follow-up — so you have one consistent eye-care relationship through the whole process.

Pre-op, surgery, post-op

What to expect.

Refractive surgery (LASIK, PRK, SMILE)

Comprehensive pre-op workup including corneal topography, dry-eye evaluation, and refractive stability check. Referral to the surgeons we trust in the region. Post-op care managed through our office at the intervals your surgeon specifies.

Cataract co-management

Cataract pre-op including biometry referrals, lens-style consultation (monofocal, multifocal, EDOF, toric), and surgeon coordination. Post-op visits at one day, one week, and one month — same office, same doctor, same chart.

Why co-management?

You see the same optometrist for years before and after the surgical event. Your records, your prescription history, your lifestyle, and your follow-up sit in one chart. The surgeon is brilliant at the surgery. We're the consistent relationship.

Good to know

Common questions.

Do you perform the surgery yourselves?

No — we co-manage. We handle your complete pre-op workup, refer you to the regional surgeons we trust, then manage all your post-op care back here in the same chair, with the same doctor and chart.

Am I a candidate for LASIK?

That's what the workup answers: corneal topography, prescription stability, and a dry-eye evaluation. Some people are better suited to PRK or should treat dry eye first — we'll tell you straight.

Which cataract replacement lens should I choose?

It depends on how you live — monofocal, multifocal, EDOF, and toric lenses all trade differently between distance, near, and night vision. We walk you through it before you ever meet the surgeon.
