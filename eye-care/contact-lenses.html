Contact Lenses & Specialty Fittings

Standard soft-contact fittings, plus specialty fittings for hard-to-fit corneas: scleral, hybrid, custom multifocal.

Standard contacts plus specialty work

What to expect.

Standard contact lens fittings

Daily disposables, bi-weeklies, monthlies, and toric lenses for astigmatism. Brand range covers Acuvue, Alcon, Bausch + Lomb, CooperVision. Annual supplies can be shipped directly to your door.

Scleral lenses

Large-diameter lenses that vault over the cornea and rest on the white of the eye. The right choice for keratoconus, post-LASIK ectasia, severe dry eye, and irregular corneal shapes that won't tolerate standard contacts.

Hybrid lenses

A rigid center surrounded by a soft skirt — clarity of a rigid lens with the comfort of a soft. Excellent for moderate keratoconus and clients who want the optics of a rigid without the adaptation curve.

Custom multifocal lenses

For presbyopic patients who want to keep wearing contacts. Dr. Carrie specializes in fitting these — multiple geometries, multiple add powers, multiple iterations until the fit is right.

Annual supply program

Buy a year's supply and your follow-up visits are at no additional charge. Lenses ship directly to you. Training included if you're new to contact lens wear.

Good to know

Common questions.

I've been told I'm “hard to fit.” Can you still wear contacts?

Almost certainly. Scleral lenses, hybrid lenses, and custom multifocals are Dr. Carrie's specialty — keratoconus, post-LASIK, severe dry eye, and irregular corneas are exactly what these designs exist for.

How does the annual supply program work?

Buy a year's supply and your follow-up visits cost nothing extra, your lenses ship straight to your door, and replacement lenses are included. New wearers get insertion and removal training too.

I've never worn contacts. Where do I start?

With a fitting appointment. We measure your eyes, pick the right lens design, and coach you through insertion and removal until you're confident — training is part of the fitting, not an upsell.
