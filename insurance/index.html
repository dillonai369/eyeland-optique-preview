What we accept

Insurance & Benefits.

We accept most major vision and medical insurance plans — and if we're out of network with yours, we do all the paperwork for you.

Vision insurance accepted

Vision plans.

VSP · EyeMed · and many regional plans.

We verify your benefits before your appointment so there are no surprises. Most exam appointments are fully covered by your vision plan; frames and lenses typically have an allowance and discount structure that we'll explain when you come in.

Medical insurance accepted

Medical plans.

Aetna · BCBS (Blue Cross Blue Shield) · Humana · Cigna · MetLife · Medicare · and others.

Medical insurance applies when your visit is for a medical concern — dry eye, ocular surface disease, diabetic retinal evaluation, cataract pre-op, glaucoma monitoring, or any acute eye condition.

Out of network?

We do the paperwork.

If your plan isn't one we're contracted with, don't let that stop you. We prepare and file the out-of-network claim on your behalf — you shop the way every other client does, and the reimbursement comes to you.

Our guarantees

How we stand behind your eyewear.

One-time exchange on any eyewear purchase within 60 days · Minimum one-year manufacturer-defect warranty on every frame · Free adjustments, always · Free on-site minor repairs — loose screws, nose pads, cleaning — whether you bought your frames from us or not.
