Varilux Progressive Lenses

The category-defining progressive lens. Distance, intermediate, near — all in one lens, with no visible line.

Why Varilux

Varilux invented the modern progressive lens in 1959 and has refined it every generation since. The current Varilux Physio X design uses wavefront technology to deliver sharper edges, faster adaptation, and a wider intermediate channel than previous generations.

Who it's for

Anyone over 40 who's noticed reading menus, phones, or fine print is getting harder. Anyone currently wearing readers and a separate pair for distance. Anyone tired of switching glasses.

Pairs well with

Crizal Sapphire anti-reflective coating for the cleanest visual experience.

Good to know

Common questions.

Is Varilux worth the price over standard progressives?

For most wearers, yes — the wider corridors and faster adaptation are noticeable daily. We'll tell you honestly if a mid-tier design would serve your prescription just as well.

Can I get Varilux with Transitions or polarized tint?

Yes — Varilux designs combine with Transitions light-adaptive material and with sun tints, so one prescription can cover indoors and out.
