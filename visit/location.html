The neighborhood

About the Fresh Market Shoppes.

The Fresh Market Shoppes sits on William Hilton Parkway just inside the south end of the island. It anchors a small shopping center built around the Fresh Market grocery, with parking out front and easy access from US-278 in both directions.

If you're coming from off-island via the Cross Island Parkway, head south on US-278 and look for the Fresh Market sign on your right. From inside the island the shop is fifteen minutes from Sea Pines, ten from Palmetto Dunes, twelve from Shelter Cove.

Visiting for vacation and broke your glasses? Walk in. Billy has rescued more than one ruined-glasses-on-day-one trip. Saturday-morning emergencies are a specialty.
