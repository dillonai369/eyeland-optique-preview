About

Why clients ask for Jeanine.

Jeanine is ABO-certified and one of the first people you'll meet when you walk into the shop. She specializes in personal-shopping appointments — the relaxed kind where you're trying on twenty frames and narrowing them down to two over the course of an hour.

More about Jeanine

The full story.

Her superpower is matching frame shape to face geometry without making you feel measured. She can tell you in about thirty seconds why a frame that looked great in your hand looks all wrong on your face, and what to swap it for.

She's worked the floor with Billy for years and has fitted some of the most particular eyewear clients on the island.

A few questions

In their own words.

Specialty?

Personal-shopping appointments. Frame styling for clients who walk in without a clear preference.

Best part of the job?

When someone tries on the third or fourth frame and you can see the moment it clicks for them.

Hardest frame fit?

A narrow PD with a strong prescription. There's a small list of houses that solve this — Lindberg, Lunor, Orgreen. We have all three.
